# Sources, attribution and reuse

This folder contains curated factual audit tables, bibliographic URLs, checksums
and original explanatory documentation. It does not contain third-party news
articles, report PDFs, photographs or screenshots.

- **Banco Central do Brasil Focus data:** the
  [official catalog](https://dadosabertos.bcb.gov.br/dataset/expectativas-mercado)
  specifies [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
  The Focus-derived database portions retain that license and BCB attribution.
- **BCB website information:** the
  [site terms](https://www.bcb.gov.br/acessoinformacao/politicaprivacidade)
  permit reproduction with source attribution and preservation of information
  integrity. Do not infer an SGS-series-specific license from another series.
- **IBGE release facts:** the table retains official Agência IBGE Notícias URLs
  and release identifiers. The
  [agency's reuse notice](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-sala-de-imprensa/2080-agencia-de-noticias/an-artigos-diversos/9277-licenca.html)
  sets attribution requirements for reproducing its content, including authors
  and image credits where applicable. This export contains dates, numeric facts
  and references, without article text or images. Raw SIDRA/calendar snapshots
  are not included and are not relabeled as ODbL or MIT here.
- **Other news sources:** their article text and images are not redistributed.
  Factual publication metadata and links support the audit. A link or checksum
  does not grant permission to reproduce the linked material.

The source publishers do not endorse this research. Preserve source attribution
and applicable notices when reusing these tables. The repository's MIT software
license does not replace third-party data/content terms.
